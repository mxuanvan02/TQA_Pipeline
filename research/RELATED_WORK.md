# Related Work Notes (Seed List)

Use this as a starting map for the paper's Related Work section.

## Legal NLP benchmarks and datasets

- LexGLUE: benchmark for legal language understanding.
- LegalBench: broad legal reasoning benchmark with diverse tasks.
- CUAD: contract understanding benchmark.
- COLIEE shared tasks: legal retrieval/entailment and statute-related tasks.

## Vietnamese legal QA resources

- ALQAC 2021 resources and competition materials.
- VLQA (Vietnamese legal QA) and related open efforts.

## Synthetic data generation

- Self-Instruct style instruction/data synthesis.
- Prompt-constrained QA generation pipelines.

## LLM-as-a-judge reliability

- MT-Bench / Chatbot Arena style studies on evaluator bias.
- G-Eval and follow-up works on judge consistency and brittleness.

## Multimodal/document QA

- DocVQA, ChartQA, and document-grounded QA setups.
- OCR quality impact on downstream QA reliability.

## Writing guidance

- In the final paper, include only sources you directly used in design/evaluation.
- Separate "inspiration references" from "directly compared methods."
